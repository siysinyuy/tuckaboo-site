Questions and answers

How do I start my free trial?

Open any game, or tap Generate Story, and choose Start 7-Day Free Trial. A grown-up answers a short question (a number written in words, typed in digits), then Apple asks you to confirm. New subscribers get 7-day free, then $5.99 per month in US dollars. Prices in other countries appear in the App Store before you confirm.

How do I cancel?

Cancel at any time in your Apple ID subscription settings: on iPhone, iPad and Apple Vision Pro open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions; on a Mac open the App Store and choose your account. To avoid being charged when the free trial ends, cancel at least 24 hours before it ends. You can also tap Manage on the Premium screen inside the app.

I already subscribed, or I reinstalled the app.

Open the Premium screen and tap Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple ID that you subscribed with.

Can I get a refund?

Apple handles all payments and refunds, so we cannot refund you ourselves. You can ask Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

What does Premium include?

Creating new stories, and the five learning games: Spelling Tiles, Sleepy Pairs, Toy Box Counting, Pattern Train and What Happened Next? Stories you have already saved can always be read, narrated and browsed, even without a subscription.

What are African Stories?

It is one of the nine places a story can happen, and it works a little differently from the others. Choose it and Tuckaboo retells a traditional African folktale, framed as an elder telling it under the acacia tree while your child and their animal friend listen. The lesson you pick chooses the tale: perseverance brings Hare and Tortoise, sharing brings Anansi and the pot of wisdom, friendship brings why the Sun and the Moon live in the sky, and so on through all ten lessons. They are retold gently for bedtime, so the races, tricks and mishaps of the older versions are softened and everyone is still friends at the end.

Can I choose where the story happens?

Yes. On the story screen, pick from a magical forest, an underwater kingdom, a space adventure, an enchanted castle, a jungle, a city in the clouds, a peaceful farm, a mountain peak, or African Stories. Each one has its own scenery, its own sounds and its own drawings.

Does Tuckaboo need the internet?

No. Stories are written on your device and games work offline. The internet is used only for the subscription: to show the price and to check that Premium is active.

Which devices does it work on?

iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Vision Pro running the latest system software (version 27). Stories written by Apple Intelligence need a device that supports it. On other devices Tuckaboo writes stories from its own gentle templates, so it still works.

Where is my child’s information kept?

Only on your device. It is never sent to us. Read the Privacy Policy for details.

How do I delete my child’s information?

Swipe a story in the Library and choose delete. Delete a child in the Profiles tab to remove their stories and game progress. Deleting the app removes everything from the device.

A story had something in it that was not suitable.

We are sorry. Every story goes through automatic safety checks, but no check is perfect. Please delete the story and email us what was wrong (copying the sentence helps) so we can improve the checks.

Can the narrator use Siri’s voice? It sounds robotic.

Apple does not let apps use Siri’s own voice. You can get close: open Narrator Voice (in the story menu, or at the bottom of the Games tab) and choose a voice, then download an Enhanced or Premium voice in your device’s Settings under Accessibility, Spoken Content, Voices. It will appear in the list, and Automatic picks the most natural voice installed.

What ages are the games for?

Ages 2 to 12. Each child starts at a level that suits their age, and the games move up or down as they play, so they are challenged but not stuck.

Is Tuckaboo accessible?

Tuckaboo works with VoiceOver, Dynamic Type and Reduce Motion, and the games can be played by tapping as well as dragging. If something does not work well for you, please tell us.
