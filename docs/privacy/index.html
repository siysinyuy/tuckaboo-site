Privacy Policy

Last updated 9 October 2026

The short version

  • Tuckaboo does not collect personal information. Everything you or your child enter stays on your device, or in your own private iCloud if a grown-up turns on iCloud sync. We never receive it.
  • There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking, and no third-party code in the app.
  • Only two things use the internet: the subscription, which Apple handles, and iCloud sync, which is off unless a grown-up turns it on. Both go to Apple, never to us. We never see your name, Apple ID or payment details.
  • You are in control. Delete a story, a child profile, or the app, and the information is gone.

Who we are

Tuckaboo is made by bahd tech (“we”, “us”). You can reach us at siysinyuy@gmail.com. This policy explains what Tuckaboo does with information. Because the app keeps everything on your device and sends nothing to us, this is mostly a description of what stays with you.

What Tuckaboo keeps on your device

To personalize stories and remember progress, the app stores the following in its private storage on your device. None of it is sent to us or to anyone else.

WhatDetails
Child profilesA first name or nickname, an age, a favorite animal, a favorite color, interests, and an avatar emoji, all typed in by a parent to personalize stories.

PreferencesText size, night mode, story backdrop, narrator voice and speed, and whether game sounds are on.
Subscription statusWhether Tuckaboo Premium is active, which the app reads from Apple’s App Store on your device.

How stories are written

Stories are written on your device. On devices that support Apple Intelligence, the app uses Apple’s on-device language model, which runs entirely on the device. On other devices, the app builds stories from gentle story templates we wrote. In both cases the name and choices you enter are used only to make the story on your device. If you choose a language other than English, the story is first written and checked in English, then translated on the device with the same on-device model, and the translation is checked again. Every story passes through these automatic safety checks on the device. Those checks are strong but not perfect, so we suggest a grown-up reads a story before sharing it with a child.

Reading aloud

The narrator uses the speech voices built into your device, and it runs on the device. If you download a more natural voice, you do that in your device’s own Settings; the app does not download anything.

Recording your own voice

A grown-up can record themselves reading a story, for nights they can’t be there. The app asks for permission to use the microphone the first time, and uses it only while a grown-up is recording. The recording is stored with the story on your device (and in your own private iCloud if sync is on). It is never sent to us. Deleting the story deletes its recording.

iCloud sync (optional)

iCloud sync is off until a grown-up turns it on in the app’s Grown-ups area, which is behind the grown-up question. When it is on, child profiles, stories and voice recordings are kept in sync between devices signed in to the same Apple Account. They are stored in your own private iCloud database, which only your Apple Account can read. We cannot see, access or download anything stored there. Game progress and preferences stay on each device and are not synced. Apple’s handling of iCloud data is described in Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Siri, Shortcuts and the widget

Tuckaboo offers Siri and Shortcuts actions such as “Start Bedtime”, and a home-screen widget that shows tonight’s story. These run on your device. The child’s name and story title are shown to whoever can see the device, so choose whether to add the widget accordingly.

Subscriptions and payments

Tuckaboo Premium is sold through Apple’s App Store. Apple handles payment, billing, the free trial, renewals, cancellations and refunds. We do not see or store your name, email address, Apple ID or payment details. The app asks Apple on your device whether the subscription is active and receives a confirmation. Apple gives us summary sales reports (for example, how many subscriptions started in a country) that do not identify you or your child. Apple’s own handling of your information is described in Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Analytics and crash reports

We do not run any analytics or crash reporting of our own. If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your device settings, Apple may pass us anonymous crash reports and usage statistics. These cannot be used to identify you or your child.

Children’s privacy

Tuckaboo is designed for children aged 2 to 12 to use together with a parent or guardian. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children, and the app is built so that there is nothing to collect: there are no accounts, no chat, no social features and no advertising. That means there is nothing for a parent to consent to, review or ask us to delete on our side.

To keep small children safe, the app asks a grown-up to answer a question (a number written in words, to be typed in digits) before any purchase, before opening a website, and before sharing a story. If a grown-up chooses to share a story, the app opens your device’s share sheet, and the story text goes wherever that grown-up chooses. That step is outside the app and outside our control.

Third parties

We do not share information with third parties, because we do not have any. The app contains no third-party software development kits, advertising or analytics code. The app’s network activity is limited to Apple’s App Store services for the subscription and, only if a grown-up turns it on, Apple’s iCloud for sync.

This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) when you visit it. The Tuckaboo app never contacts this website by itself: it opens only if a grown-up chooses to view these pages.

How long information is kept, and deleting it

Information stays on your device until you remove it. You can:

  • delete a story from the Library (swipe it and choose delete);
  • delete a child profile in the Profiles tab, which also deletes that child’s stories and game progress;
  • delete the app, which removes all of its information from the device.

When iCloud sync is on, deleting a story or a child profile also deletes it from iCloud and from your other devices. Turning sync off stops syncing but does not remove what is already in your iCloud; you can delete Tuckaboo’s iCloud data from your device’s iCloud storage settings.

Depending on your settings, your device’s own backups (for example iCloud Backup or a backup on your computer) may contain a copy of app information. Those backups are managed by you and Apple, not by us.

Security

Information is kept in the app’s private storage on your device, which is protected by your device’s passcode and encryption. If iCloud sync is on, the synced copy is protected by Apple’s iCloud security in your private database. We run no servers that hold your information, so there is nothing on our side that could be breached.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to see, correct or delete information about you, or to object to how it is used. Because we hold none of your information (anything synced is in your own iCloud, not with us), you can already do all of this yourself in the app, as described above. If you have a question or a request, write to siysinyuy@gmail.com and we will help.

Changes to this policy

If we ever change how Tuckaboo handles information, for example by adding a feature that sends information off the device, we will update this policy and the App Store privacy details before that version is released, and we will ask for permission where the law requires it. The date at the top shows when this policy last changed.
