Tuckaboo

Calm bedtime stories and learning games, made for your child.

Tuckaboo writes a personalized bedtime story for your child, on your device, and adds five learning games: spelling, memory, counting, patterns and story order.

A story that belongs to your child

Tell Tuckaboo your child’s name and age, the animal friend they love, their favorite color and what they are into this week. Choose a gentle lesson and a place for the story to happen, and Tuckaboo writes it — slow, warm and unhurried, winding down a little more with every paragraph until it reaches goodnight.

Nine worlds, including African folktales

Stories can happen in a magical forest, an underwater kingdom, among the stars, in an enchanted castle, a sunlit jungle, a city in the clouds, on a peaceful farm, or high on a quiet mountain.

Choose African Stories and the evening becomes something else: an elder under the acacia tree, telling a tale as the cooking fires burn low. Hare and Tortoise walking to the far baobab. Anansi and the pot of wisdom. How the Sun and the Moon came to live in the sky. The honeyguide bird and its share of the honey. Ten traditional tales, retold gently for bedtime, with the races, tricks and mishaps softened so everyone is still friends at the end.

Ten gentle lessons

Kindness, honesty, courage, friendship, perseverance, sharing, patience, gratitude, responsibility and empathy — each one shown through what the characters do, never delivered as a lecture.

Written on your device

On devices that support Apple Intelligence, stories are written by Apple’s on-device language model. On every other device, Tuckaboo writes them from gentle story templates instead — so it always works, with or without Apple Intelligence, and with or without the internet.

Either way, nothing your child tells Tuckaboo is ever sent to us. There is no server to send it to. If a grown-up turns on iCloud sync, profiles, stories and recordings go only to the family’s own private iCloud, which we can’t see.

Safety, taken seriously

Everything a grown-up types is checked before it reaches a story, and every finished story is reviewed again before a child can see it — for anything frightening, unkind, upsetting or simply too lively for bedtime. A story that does not pass is never shown; Tuckaboo quietly writes a different one. No automatic check is perfect, so we still suggest a grown-up reads along.

A grown-up check — a number written in words, to be typed in digits — stands in front of every purchase, every link out to the web, and every share.
